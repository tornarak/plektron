## summary

Two documentaries show the herding Bogoras describes in "Reindeer-Breeding",
a century on. [[tundra]] follows the herder Vukvukai and a herd of some 14,000
on the Bering Sea coast. [[reindeer-people]] is shorter and set in winter: the
calves are separated out and the weak animals sorted from the strong, and the
herders speak of the reindeer-mother who protects the camp.

## full

[[tundra]] is the longer witness and the one a reader without Russian can
follow, since it carries English subtitles over Russian and Chukchi speech.
[[reindeer-people]] runs under half an hour.
