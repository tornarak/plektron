## summary

Two documentaries show the sea hunting of the Bering Strait coast that
[[people:maritime-chukchi]] villages still live by. [[book-of-the-sea]] is set
in Lorino, among Chukchi and Yupik whale and walrus hunters. [[hunters]] follows
a Chukchi hunting community from winter to summer through fish, seal and whale.

## full

[[book-of-the-sea]] cuts between the hunt and clay animation of Yupik myth,
among them the woman who gave birth to a whale. It is a paid rental.

[[hunters]] has little dialogue and is mostly visual.
