## summary

Bogoras describes the shamanic séance in the inner room at length and could film
none of it. [[film]] — Andrei Golovnev's 32-minute documentary on
[[people:reindeer-chukchi]] herders of the Pegtymel River — includes a séance,
and is perhaps the only good footage of one there is.

## full

The film puts a camp's daily round beside an autumn ritual, fly-agaric stories
told by the elder Natalko, and the rock paintings on the river cliffs, roughly a
thousand years old. Each has a place in the book: the séance in
"Shamanistic Performance in the Inner Room", the autumn rite in "Fall
Ceremonial", and the fly agaric in "Intoxicants".

The film reportedly won the Grand Prix at the 2000 Russian Anthropological Film
Festival. It is streamed from Golovnev's own Culture Unplugged account, where he
is credited as director and producer.
