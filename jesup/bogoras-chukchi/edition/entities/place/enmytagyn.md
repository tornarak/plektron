## gloss

Located by the editor from Bogoras's gazetteer (1-048 to 1-053), cross-referenced against Operational Navigation Charts and OpenStreetMap. Bogoras + ONC; latter attests as "Enmytagyn".

**This is the Arctic-coast one.** 1-052 lists two villages under that gloss —
this one as Ot-ᴇnmɪtaˊhɪn, "cliff's end of Uˊten", in the stretch where "all the
villages belong to the Chukchee tribe," and a Naukan one as bare ᴇnmɪtaˊhɪn on
the Pacific side, which is [[place:dezhnyovo]]. The `Ot-` prefix is the only
thing in print that tells them apart.
