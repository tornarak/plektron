## gloss

The Eskimo of the American side of the strait, including the Diomede Islanders.
[[land:alaska]] is Kɪˊɪmɪn in Chukchee, so its people are **Kɪˊɪmɪlɪt**; they are
also **Roˊčhɪlɪt**, "opposite-shore people"; and also **Yɪkɪrg̣aˊulɪt**, "the
[large] mouthed", from yɪkɪˊrg̣ɪn "mouth" — 1-041 says the labrets they wore
"having made their mouths their most conspicuous feature".

**The labrets are what identify them.** 1-041 attaches Yɪkɪrg̣aˊulɪt to the
Diomede Islanders in one sentence and to the inhabitants of the American
continent in the next, which reads like two peoples until the ornament is
noticed: labret-wearing is the Bering Strait Iñupiat's, and the Chukchee are
naming one people by the thing that distinguishes them from the Eskimo on their
own shore. So these are exonyms filed here rather than three entries of their
own.
