## gloss

**The one disease in the section whose Chukchee name says in so many words that
it is a Being.** Bogoras parses aˊtal-vaˊɪrg̣ɪn on chukchi-1-063 as aˊtal “Chuvantzy” +
vaˊɪrg̣ɪn “substance” — vaˊɪrg̣ɪn being the word chukchi-2-040 heads a section with,
“Other ‘Beings’” (vaˊɪrg̣ɪt), and that chukchi-1-073 glosses the other way in the same
breath: qaˊa-vaˊɪrg̣ɪn, “literally, ‘reindeer substance’”. The name is *the
Chuvan Being*, and it is why the section's diseases are filed among the Beings.

The etymology is also an aetiology, and Bogoras says so: the Chukchee “regard it
as indigenous”, but the name points at the [[people:chuvans]], “the tribe who in
earlier times were mediators between the [[people:russians]] and the Chukchee,
and probably, after obtaining the disease from the Russians, introduced it among
the Chukchee.”
