## gloss

The epidemic Bogoras watched. Brought from Vladivostok by a Russian trader in
1899, north through the summer of 1900 into the Chukchee villages and camps,
over to America in 1901; several thousand dead in all (chukchi-1-062, chukchi-1-063).

The mortality is a virgin-soil epidemic's, and Bogoras's explanation is the
clinical one: it “requires the utmost caution against colds during
convalescence, and the conditions of arctic life make this practically
impossible.” No Chukchee name given — see [[being:small-pox]] on the class.
