## gloss

The Raven of the tales and the incantations.

**Not the Creator, and not Koryak Big-Raven.** chukchi-2-041 is explicit on both counts:
among the Koryak the Creator "is often identified with the Big-Raven ... who
represents the chief Deity. In Chukchee tales, on the contrary, the Raven ...
even in his name, plays a less important part, and is almost always
distinguished from the Creator." Where he does loom larger — the Telqäˊp tundra
and Big-River camps — Bogoras reads Koryak influence. Big-Raven is a separate
figure under a separate name, and this entry is not about him.

**And a distinction the book records without settling.** chukchi-2-045: one shaman
"distinguished between this Raven and the Raven who restored to the earth the
sources of light" — the first an "assistant" to the Creator or the Zenith, who
lives near the Polar Star and devours disease as a bird devours worms; the
second a "very mighty 'Being'" who dealt with mankind only at the creation and
afterwards "transformed himself into thunder, and became invisible." One
informant's ruling, reported here and not adopted.
