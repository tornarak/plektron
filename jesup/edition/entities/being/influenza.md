## gloss

“A kind of grippe or influenza” (chukchi-1-063), coming from the south and the west,
“and therefore must be considered as coming from the Russian settlements”
(chukchi-1-064).

**The one disease in the section Bogoras reports being read as a spirit
outright.** In 1896, on the Upper Anui, the Chaun people heard of cases on the
[[river:keperveyem]] and fled the whole western part of the Chaun tundra to
avoid “the spirits seeking for prey”.
