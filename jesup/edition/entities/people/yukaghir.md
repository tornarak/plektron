## gloss

The Chukchee's westerly neighbors - hunters, fishers and herders - much apter than the Chukchee to fraternize with Russians. When the Cossacks withdrew the [[people:chukchi]] slaughtered the Yukaghir en masse and usurped much of their former territory.

An important subgroup is the [[people:chuvans]]
