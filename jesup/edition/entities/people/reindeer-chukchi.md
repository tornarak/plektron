## gloss

The inland, reindeer-breeding division of the [[people:chukchi]], as against the
coastal [[people:maritime-chukchi]]. Bogoras treats the division as the
organising fact of Chukchee material life and states on chukchi-1-021 that their material
life “is quite uniform throughout” the whole inland range, from central Kamchatka
and the Upper Omolon to the Arctic coast.

**Coextensive with [[people:chukchi]] for the atlas's purposes**, so the map is
drawn at the parent's extent rather than at a boundary nobody has drawn. That is
an editorial call and not a source's: the Reindeer Chukchi occupy the inland
whole of the Chukchee range, which is the range, where
[[people:maritime-chukchi]] occupy a coastal strip of it that no source in hand
delineates.
