## gloss

The racial type, never the Caucasus: all three occurrences stand for Europeans in
general, and the book has no occasion to mention the mountains or the peoples who
live in them.

Bogoras uses it as his baseline of comparison in chapter II, and each time to
narrow a difference his readers would have assumed — Chukchee eyes are straight
and often as large, the body hair is scarcely distinguishable (1-056). 3-060 puts
it to a different use, reaching for the scarcity of "blood really Russian and
Caucasian" to explain why marriages between Russians and Chukchee on the Kolyma
are childless.
