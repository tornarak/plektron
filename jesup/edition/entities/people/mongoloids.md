## gloss

The racial type of turn-of-the-century anthropometry, and in this book a
description of features rather than of anyone: a nose with a low bridge (1-055),
a face "strongly Mongolian in outline," which Bogoras reports as commoner among
the women than the men (1-056). The word is never a name for a group of people
here — it modifies a nose or a profile.

What the category is worth, and what this store does and does not claim by
recording it, is [[people:physical-anthropology]]. The [[people:mongols]] are a
real people and a separate entry; the book spells both *Mongolian*.
