## gloss

"Kihiˊni Island" (chukchi-1-048), "an island of the same name" as the village on it
(chukchi-1-052), "the island of Kihiˊni" (chukchi-1-160). It carries two of the gazetteer's
villages — [[place:kihini]] on the northwestern shore and [[place:yergin]] at the
southwestern end — and both points fall inside the traced shape, which is the
text and the tracing agreeing without having been asked to.

No gazetteer in hand draws it, so the area on the map is the editor's own tracing.
