## gloss

Медвежьи острова, off the mouth of the [[river:kolyma]]. Bogoras cites the
ruins on them three times (chukchi-1-037, chukchi-1-038, chukchi-1-230) as evidence about the
[[people:yukaghir]] and about earth-covered hut construction. Filed under the
region and not under the [[river:kolyma]]: they are in the sea off its mouth,
not in its basin, and `within:` is what the atlas draws membership from.
