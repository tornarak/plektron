## gloss

**A range, held here by one point and a frame.** Bogoras's map runs it
east-southeast from about [[place:chukchee-fair]] to the head of the
[[river:opuka]]; the coordinate is the midpoint of that run and the map's frame
is the run itself.

The upland between the [[river:anadyr]] and the Pacific watersheds, the range
being the divide itself.
