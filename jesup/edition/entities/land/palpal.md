## gloss

**A range, held here by one point and a frame.** Bogoras's map runs it
east-southeast from about [[place:chukchee-fair]] to the head of the
[[river:opuka]]; the coordinate is the midpoint of that run and the `frame:`
is the run itself, so a map of it shows a range rather than zooming to a
peak the book never names. The eastern end is read off `watershed:opuka`'s
own westernmost vertex — 172.437, 62.296 — which is where the store already
puts the river's source. An earlier point sat half a degree north of the fair
at the fair's longitude, off the line entirely.

The upland between the [[river:anadyr]] and the Pacific watersheds — which is
the same fact the position rests on, the range being the divide itself. Bogoras
names it as country that had no inhabitants at all before the Chukchee took it
(chukchi-1-035), as the summering ground the reindeer leave in mid-March (chukchi-1-175), and
as one end of a migration route running to the Chaun tundra (chukchi-1-174).
