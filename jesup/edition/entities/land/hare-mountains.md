## gloss

A low, broad range east of the middle and upper [[river:kanchalan]] — the
editor’s identification. The book names the same feature twice and never says so:
“Hare Mountains” with Ушканій хребетъ beside it in chukchi-1-359’s Anadyr Country list,
and “the Kanchalan Hills” at chukchi-1-158.

The proper name leads because Ушканій хребетъ *is* one — “Hare Ridge” — where
“Kanchalan Hills” describes the range by the river it stands over.

The coordinate is the editor’s, and a point standing for a range; it falls inside
the Kanchalan's basin.
