## gloss

**The entry is the region and the shape is the peninsula, and they are not the
same size.** The outline on the map is Natural Earth's `KAMCHATKA PENINSULA`,
which stops at the isthmus; the book's Kamchatka reaches well north of it —
chukchi-1-359's own section heading is "Northern Kamchatka and Adjacent Pacific Shore",
and Bogoras files Tiličiki, Olyutorka and the Olyutorsky capes under it.

So a dozen places filed here sit outside the outline — [[river:pakhacha]] and
[[place:cape-olutorsky]] among them. That is correct: one is the book's scope, the
other a source's polygon, and inventing a boundary for "Kamchatka the region" is
the one thing this atlas refuses to do.
