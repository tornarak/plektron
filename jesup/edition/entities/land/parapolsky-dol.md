## gloss

Bogoras defines it outright, in a footnote at chukchi-1-035: **“The high flat country
between Penshina and Baron Korff’s Bays.”** The isthmus joining
[[land:kamchatka]] to the mainland, bounded by [[water:penzhina-bay]] on the
Okhotsk side and [[water:korf-bay]] on the Bering side.

**The map's frame is measured from the two bays he names**, not estimated. No
source draws the Dol, so the frame says where to look, not where a boundary lies.
