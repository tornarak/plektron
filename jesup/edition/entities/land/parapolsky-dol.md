## gloss

Bogoras defines it outright, in a footnote at chukchi-1-035: **“The high flat country
between Penshina and Baron Korff’s Bays.”** The isthmus joining
[[land:kamchatka]] to the mainland, bounded by [[water:penzhina-bay]] on the
Okhotsk side and [[water:korf-bay]] on the Bering side.

He uses it as Chukchee-Koryak border country — emigrant Chukchee interspersed
among the Koryak as far as the Dol (chukchi-1-035), camps scattered on it along the
Koryak border (chukchi-1-046), an ancient fair in its south on [[river:anapka]]
(chukchi-1-076), and a spring fair for the southwestern Chukchee near the upper
[[river:opuka]] (chukchi-1-080).

**The `frame:` is measured from the two bays he names**, not estimated: it is
`geo bounds water:penzhina-bay water:korf-bay --margin 0.6`. No source draws
the Dol, so the entry stays unlocated — a frame says where to look, not where a
thing is — but the viewport is derived from the definition rather than from
anybody's idea of where it ought to sit.
