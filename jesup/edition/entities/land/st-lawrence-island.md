## gloss

The island in the northern Bering Sea, off the mouth of the [[river:anadyr]]
and about 60 km from the Chukotka coast. The book meets it as the other end of
a trade route: Chukchee crews go over in boats for reindeer-skins, and kettles
and other goods come back from it (chukchi-1-051, chukchi-1-086, chukchi-1-104).

Filing it under [[water:bering-strait]] is an **editorial grouping, not a
hydrographic claim** — strictly the island lies in the northern Bering Sea, south
of the strait proper. It hangs there because the strait is the crossing the book
actually describes. Not to be confused with St. Lawrence Bay on the Chukotka coast,
[[water:lavrentiya-bay]].
