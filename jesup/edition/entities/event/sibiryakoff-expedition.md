## gloss

The expedition financed by the Siberian gold magnate Alexander Sibiryakoff, under
which [[person:bogoras]]’s Kolyma-district work of 1895–98 was carried out, its
material published in Russian by the Imperial Academy of Sciences (chukchi-1-021).
Distinct from the [[event:jesup-expedition]], which funded only his second journey
of 1900–01.
