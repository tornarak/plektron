## gloss

The Western constellation, used as a bearing for a Chukchee one: chukchi-2-036 names it
"the Elk-Hunter (Lynx)", chukchi-2-035 has a hunter of it with "a dog tied behind his
sledge", and chukchi-2-013 notes that one of its reindeer-drivers carries the same name
as the cat's-cradle figure "Mouse-Driver". The Chukchee constellation is the
Elk-Hunter and this is the parenthesis Bogoras glosses it with, the same
arrangement as [[sky:eagle]].

All three capitalized `Lynx` in the book are this, and none is the animal;
lowercase `lynx` is the animal, [[taxon:lynx-lynx]].
