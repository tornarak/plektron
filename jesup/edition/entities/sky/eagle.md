## gloss

Aquila, named in the Western way and used as a bearing rather than as a
constellation the Chukchee keep. chukchi-2-033: "The stars Altair and Tarared of the
constellation Eagle are combined by the Chukchee into a separate constellation",
which "is believed to usher in the light of the new year, because it begins to
appear above the horizon at the time of the winter solstice", and to which the
Reindeer Chukchee and most Maritime families sacrifice at its first appearance.
On both the Kolyma and the Anadyr Bogoras was told it was a forefather of the
tribe. chukchi-2-036 glosses a figure with the same parenthesis. The Chukchee name is
given on both pages and is not spelled here: it reaches this entry only through
OCR.

So the entry is the Western constellation Bogoras measures from, and the
Chukchee asterism cut out of it is a different object that wants its own entry
once its name is keyed. This is the same division [[sky:lynx]] records, where the
constellation Lynx carries the Chukchee Elk-Hunter.

The capitalized `Eagle` of chukchi-3-071's tale — who takes the Raven's wife and whose
son a giant eagle carries off — belongs to neither this entry nor
[[taxon:aves]], and is unminted. Lowercase `eagle` is the bird throughout: the
one at chukchi-1-253 the Maritime Chukchee will not eat, and the "giant eagle" of
supernatural strength at chukchi-2-054.
