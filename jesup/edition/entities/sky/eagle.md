## gloss

Aquila, named in the Western way and used as a bearing rather than as a
constellation the Chukchee keep. chukchi-2-033: "The stars Altair and Tarared of the
constellation Eagle are combined by the Chukchee into a separate constellation",
which "is believed to usher in the light of the new year, because it begins to
appear above the horizon at the time of the winter solstice", and to which the
Reindeer Chukchee and most Maritime families sacrifice at its first appearance.
On both the Kolyma and the Anadyr Bogoras was told it was a forefather of the
tribe.

So the entry is the Western constellation Bogoras measures from, the same division
[[sky:lynx]] records, where the constellation Lynx carries the Chukchee Elk-Hunter.

The capitalized `Eagle` of chukchi-3-071's tale, who takes the Raven's wife, appears
only there; this constellation stands for him.

**Open: the Chukchee asterism.** Cut out of this constellation, it is a different
object that wants its own entry; its name is given at chukchi-2-033 and chukchi-2-036.
