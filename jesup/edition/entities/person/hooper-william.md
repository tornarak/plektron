## gloss

Author of *Ten Months among the Tents of the Tuski* (1853), which the book cites
as *Hooper*: [[source:hooper-1853]].

**Every printed `Hooper` is this man.** Authorities Quoted also lists Calvin
L. Hooper's *Corwin* report (1881), [[source:hooper-1881]], but the text never
cites it.
