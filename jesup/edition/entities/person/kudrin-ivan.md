## gloss

A Cossack trader, "the last of these traders", who in 1882 brought W. H. Gilder
to the Kolyma and "in a way wanted to take him captive" (chukchi-1-080). Dead by the
writing of chukchi-1-085, where he "had the reputation of being out of his mind". His
brother is [[person:kudrin-theodore]]. `Vanker` is the Chukchee form Gilder
prints, which Bogoras calls "a corruption of his two [names]". See
[[person:gilder]], [[people:cossacks]].
