## gloss

"In the middle of the seventeenth century, Deshneff, in his well-known report
to the government about his travels and discoveries, mentions, in connection
with the Chuvantzy, the tribe or the clan of the Anauly" (chukchi-1-038, footnote).
Identifying him with Semyon Dezhnyov, whose name the modern chart carries at
[[place:dezhnyovo]], is the obvious reading, not the book's own statement. See
[[people:chuvans]].
