## gloss

Гижигинская губа. The head of one of chukchi-1-359's gazetteer tables, and third of the
three things the book calls Gishiga: the river [[river:gizhiga]], the town
[[place:gizhiginsk]] at its mouth, and this.

**Not inside [[water:penzhina-bay]].** The two are siblings: both are arms of
Shelikhov Gulf, the northeastern reach of the Sea of Okhotsk, with the Taygonos
peninsula between them. Filed under [[water:okhotsk-sea]] because Bogoras never
names Shelikhov Gulf.
