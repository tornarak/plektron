## gloss

[[person:jochelson]]’s *The Koryak* — Memoirs AMNH Vol. X, and Vol. VI of the
Jesup series this book is Vol. VII of. **Not in Authorities Quoted**: part 1
went to press in 1904 and the work did not exist yet, so folio 9's asterism —
"For other authorities see references in the text" — is the only provision the
print makes for it.

Parts 2 and 3 cite it as "Vol. VI, Jochelson, The Koryak, p. NN" — the `Vol. VI`
is the *series* volume and stands before the name. Bare *the Koryak* is the
people, [[people:koryak]].
