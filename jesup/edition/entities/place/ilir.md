## gloss

A village of northern [[land:kamchatka]], from chukchi-1-359's list, where the Russian
equivalent is printed as Култусно.

**Not located, and the near miss is recorded so nobody makes it twice.**
GeoNames has `Kultushino` (Култушино, 2124032) at 60.472 166.287 — in the right
district, a few kilometres from [[place:tilliran]] and [[place:alut]], which
chukchi-1-359 lists either side of it. But Култусно and Култушино differ by more than
the 1918 reform accounts for, and a cluster of neighbours is corroboration for a
*region*, not for a name. The candidate is left here unadopted rather than in
the `link:` field.
