## gloss

With [[place:cape-navarin]], the southern limit of the Chukchee–Eskimo trade on
the Pacific coast (chukchi-1-093), and the southeastern end of the Kerek country, which
runs from [[place:cape-anannon]] to here (chukchi-1-039).
