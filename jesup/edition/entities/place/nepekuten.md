## gloss

Located by the editor from Bogoras's gazetteer (chukchi-1-048 to chukchi-1-053), traced in QGIS.
Bogoras only. chukchi-1-051 puts it "on the island called Ipeˊkut in Chukchee", which is
[[land:ipekut]]; the point falls inside the traced shape, so the text and the
tracing witness the containment separately.
