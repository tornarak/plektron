## gloss

The Koryak village on [[water:penzhina-bay]] whose people "have for a long time
occupied themselves with" iron-working (chukchi-1-076); named with
[[place:kamenskoye]] at chukchi-1-164, and one end of the stretch
[[person:jochelson]] travelled from [[place:gizhiginsk]] (chukchi-1-044).
