## gloss

Located by the editor on Bogoras's own map, which puts the settlement on the **west coast** of [[water:tkachen-bay]]. An earlier point, read from the gazetteer (chukchi-1-048 to chukchi-1-053) against Operational Navigation Charts and OpenStreetMap, sat about 15 km north at the head of the bay; the map is the closer witness to where Bogoras understood the place to be, and supersedes it. Bogoras only; though ONC confirms the bay name.
