## gloss

Located by the editor from Bogoras's gazetteer (chukchi-1-048 to chukchi-1-053), traced in QGIS.
Bogoras only. chukchi-1-052: 1 house, "all the other inhabitants have become
reindeer-breeders", against the Register's 3. Bogoras notes at chukchi-1-048 that
Nordquist "evidently confounds the small village Kuˊkun with the larger
Luˊᵋren", reversing the two house-counts — so a source naming this place with
20 houses is naming the other one. On the Pacific coast (chukchi-1-054).
