## gloss

The Yupik village on the northwest cape of [[land:st-lawrence-island]], and the
modern Gambell, Alaska — GeoNames still heads the settlement under the native
name and files "Gambell" as the alternate. The English name is a memorial to
Vene C. Gambell and his wife, missionaries there 1894–1898, and post-dates the
village by however long the village has stood; Bogoras writes only the native
form.

One mention: at chukchi-1-164 its inhabitants are named with the Eskimo of
[[place:chaplino]], [[place:cape-chukotsky]] and [[place:sirenik]] as the people
still whaling on the Pacific, "all use explosive harpoons" — the only place in
the whaling passage where the book crosses to the American side.

The village is also called Sivuqaq, which is the Yupik name of the **island** as
well; it is deliberately not an alias here, since a rule linking it could not
tell the village from [[land:st-lawrence-island]] and this store has no page
attesting either use.
