## gloss

The Anui fair. Held at irregular intervals "sometimes on the Dry Anui, near
[[place:mount-obrom]], and at a place called Ostrovnoye … sometimes on the
Large Anui, near the mouth of its affluent, the [[river:angarka]]. Finally
Ostrovnoye prevailed, because the Chukchee were more numerous on the Dry Anui"
(chukchi-1-078). The book glosses the name "Insular".

**Now filed on the Dry Anui**, which is [[river:maliy-anyuy]]. This entry stood
outside a river for a while, on the note that the sentence above distinguishes
the Dry Anui from the Large Anui while [[river:bolshoy-anyuy]] claimed both
names. That was the mislabel, not a subtlety: chukchi-1-078 opposes the two, chukchi-1-046 puts
the Dry Anui "between the Arctic Ocean and the Dry Anui River" — the northern
basin — and this village's own coordinate sits inside the Maliy's watershed,
with [[place:mount-obrom]], the other fair site in the same sentence, beside it.
Refusing to file it was the right call while the store disagreed with itself.
