## gloss

The Anui fair. Held at irregular intervals "sometimes on the Dry Anui, near
[[place:mount-obrom]], and at a place called Ostrovnoye … sometimes on the
Large Anui, near the mouth of its affluent, the [[river:angarka]]. Finally
Ostrovnoye prevailed, because the Chukchee were more numerous on the Dry Anui"
(chukchi-1-078). The book glosses the name "Insular".

**Filed on the Dry Anui**, which is [[river:maliy-anyuy]]: chukchi-1-078 opposes it to the
Large Anui, and this village's coordinate sits inside the Maliy's basin.
