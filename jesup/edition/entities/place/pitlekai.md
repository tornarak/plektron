## gloss

The Arctic-coast village where [[person:nordenskiold]]'s *Vega* wintered in
1878–79, and the station whose temperature record Bogoras quotes at chukchi-1-045. The
Chukchee form the book prints beside it is Piˊlhäqäi; it is left to the
lexicon rather than claimed here, the two not being the same spelling of one
word.
