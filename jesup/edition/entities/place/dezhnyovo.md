## gloss

"ᴇnmɪtaˊhɪn ('cliff's end'): 6 houses (Register, 8 houses, 42 inhabitants). The
people are Nɵˊɵkalɪt Eskimo" (chukchi-1-052) — one of the two villages on that page
Bogoras assigns to the [[people:naukans]], the other being [[place:naukan]]
itself. Located by the editor on Bogoras's own map, and corresponds to the
Soviet village of Dezhnyovo, whose name this entry takes since the settlement
outlived the book under it.

**Not [[place:enmytagyn]], which chukchi-1-052 spells Ot-ᴇnmɪtaˊhɪn, "cliff's end of
Uˊten".** Bogoras gives the same gloss to both and they are some 45 km apart on
opposite coasts: this one stands on the Pacific side south of East Cape among
Naukan people, and that one on the Arctic shore west of [[place:uelen]], in the
stretch where "all the villages belong to the Chukchee tribe." Two cliffs, two
peoples, one printed gloss — which is why the bare form here is `auto: no`.
