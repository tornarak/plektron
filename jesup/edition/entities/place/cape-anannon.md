## gloss

The southwestern end of the Kerek country, which runs from here to
[[place:cape-barykoff]] (chukchi-1-039), and the eastern limit of a line drawn from the
mouth of the [[river:indigirka]] (chukchi-1-034).

**The maps' "Cape Olutorsky" is this cape.** "The Cape Olutorsky of Russian and
American maps is in reality Cape Anannon, close to which are situated the first
southwestern villages of the Kerek; while the real Cape Olutorsky lies close to
Baron Korff's Bay (on the Russian maps, Cape Govensky)" (chukchi-1-032, repeated in
chukchi-1-359's note 2). The book's own map bears him out: ANANNON CAPE is drawn on this
headland and ALUTORSKY CAPE some 230 km west, on [[water:korf-bay]]. So the
coordinate above is the cape modern charts call Мыс Олюторский, and
`geonames:2122521` — which names that feature — hangs here rather than on
[[place:cape-olutorsky]]. That is Bogoras's identification, asserted against the
maps and carried by this edition, not a fact the GeoNames entry states.
