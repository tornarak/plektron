## gloss

Located by the editor from Bogoras's gazetteer (chukchi-1-048 to chukchi-1-053), traced in QGIS.
Bogoras only. chukchi-1-052: "on an island of the same name: 3 houses (not mentioned in
the Register)"; chukchi-1-048 puts it "on its outward northwestern shore" of
[[land:kihini]]. Named again at chukchi-1-160 beside [[place:yergin]].

**The bare form is not rule-linkable**, and that is the store recording an
ambiguity rather than a gap: chukchi-1-048 sets "On Kihiˊni Island the village Kihiˊni"
in one line, so a rule reading `Kihiˊni` would be wrong about half of it. The
Eskimo form is worse — [[place:cape-prince-of-wales]] claims `Kiˊhi` from chukchi-1-041
fn 2, and the footnote marker Bogoras hangs on `Kiˊhi¹` at chukchi-1-052 is him pointing
at that same identity. Both stay `auto: no`, and the generator offers them as
candidates for a reader to dispose of.
