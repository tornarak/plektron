## gloss

Attested by Bogoras's map: a tiny rivulet in the Kolyma basin, very close to the
coast, that does not seem to exist any more. chukchi-1-359 lists it under "The Lower
Kolyma" as Keiˊñu-weˊem / Медвѣжья рѣка, with footnote 3: "Chukchee. The meaning
is the same as in Russian" — bear, in both.
