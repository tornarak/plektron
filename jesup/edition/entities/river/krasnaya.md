## gloss

Bogoras's "Red River" is the [[river:anadyr]] tributary feeding Red Lake — озеро
Красное, the largest lake of the Anadyr lowland, named for the reddish rock of
its shore and joined to the Anadyr by two channels. That relation is what
identifies the river, which is otherwise thinly attested on modern maps; the
basin shape is `watershed:krasnaya-or-red-river`. `Красная` is the river,
feminine with `река`; `Красное` is the lake, and the geo slug read the lake's
form until this was looked at.
