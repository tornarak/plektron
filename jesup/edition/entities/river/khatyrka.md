## gloss

The river chukchi-1-359’s footnote 1 calls the proper Хатырка, east of
[[place:khatyrka]]. Hatɪˊykan and Hatɪˊrkan are its Kerek names.

**The river and the village share a name and are two entries**, which is the
footnote’s own point: the Russians apply Хатырка to both. The village's point is
at the mouth.
