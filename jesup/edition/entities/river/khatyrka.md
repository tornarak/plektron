## gloss

The river chukchi-1-359’s footnote 1 calls the proper Хатырка, east of
[[place:khatyrka]]. Hatɪˊykan and Hatɪˊrkan are its Kerek names, filed without a
language because the Tier-1 code set carries no Kerek.

**The river and the village share a name and are two entries**, which is the
footnote’s own point: the Russians apply Хатырка to both, and the form is
`auto: no` on each because no rule can tell which one a page means. The basin
carries the river now and the earlier `geo:` point is gone — a shape is the
location, and `entity check` refuses a record holding both. The village keeps
its own point at the mouth, some 200 km east of where that point sat.
