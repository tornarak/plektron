## gloss

**A fur that crossed the strait, not an animal of the country.** Bogoras settles
the identification himself at chukchi-1-078: the marten peltries and marten garments of
the Anyui fair "came from the American shore … all from America, since these
animals are not found in Asia." Every other trade mention agrees — chukchi-1-089, chukchi-3-106,
chukchi-3-162, chukchi-3-176 — and the fair's own price table at chukchi-3-171 sets "1 overcoat of marten"
and "3 martens" against so many red foxes.

The book keeps *marten* and *sable* apart throughout, and they are two animals from
two continents: the sable of [[taxon:martes-zibellina]] comes down the Kolyma, this
one comes across from Alaska.

Two mentions sit inside translated seventeenth-century Cossack reports and are not
observations. chukchi-3-156 inventories the land across the strait as holding "sables and
martens, foxes, polar foxes, wolves, wolverenes, polar bears, marine beavers
(sea-otters)" — hearsay, and no sable lives there; the pair is Russian trade
vocabulary rather than two identified animals. This entry claims the fur, not a
range.
