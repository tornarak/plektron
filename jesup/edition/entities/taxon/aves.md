## gloss

A rank the corpus never prints, carrying two English forms that name no clade
beneath it.

**`birds of prey`** (chukchi-1-252, tabooed by the Reindeer Chukchee) is polyphyletic in
the plainest way: hawks, eagles and falcons are not each other's closest
relatives, and owls are further still — [[taxon:strix-nyctea]], the one raptor
the chapter names in Latin, is the snowy owl the same sentence says *is* eaten.
The taboo and its exception therefore cannot both hang from one group.

**`eagle`** (chukchi-1-253, "as the eagle and the raven. To kill the former would bring a
dense fog") is polyphyletic within Accipitridae, and the page names no species.
The white-tailed eagle and the golden eagle both occur in the country; nothing
here says which, or whether the distinction is one Bogoras's informants drew.

Both sit at this rank because the coarsest honest placement is the rule
`docs/edition.md` sets for a folk group. [[taxon:corvus-corax]], the raven of the
same sentence, needs no such treatment — it is one bird.
