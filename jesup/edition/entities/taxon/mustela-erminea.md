## gloss

**Trapped as vermin, invoked as a guardian.** 1-184 sets it among the small game
taken with snares and mouse-traps, by a self-acting bow (Fig. 52) sprung on the
animal's track; 2-067 has it preferred above other amulets "because it is so nimble
and active," and 2-009 has it appear "as a stately warrior clad in white armor."
The white winter coat, the speed, and the mouse-killing are all one animal.

The book never sets *weasel*, and the binomial is not printed anywhere. The species
is circumpolar, so the Asiatic mentions and the American ones need no split.
