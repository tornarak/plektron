## gloss

The cod family. **The grouping is the book's**, though the name is not: chukchi-1-191's
wachna footnote reads "This name is applied to various species of Gadi; such as
Boreogadus polaris in the Arctic, Microgadus proximus and Eleginus navaga
(Gadus wachna Pall.) in the Pacific Ocean" — four genera named as one kind, in
one sentence, under one Chukchee and Russian word (veˊqän, Вахня). *Gadi* is
the plural of [[taxon:gadus]] and is filed as that genus's alias, not as this
family's name; nothing in the corpus prints `Gadidae`, which is why this entry
has no alias row at all.
