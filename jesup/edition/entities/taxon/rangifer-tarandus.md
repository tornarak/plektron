## gloss

Wild and domesticated reindeer are one species; the book treats them as two
conditions of it, and nothing here splits them. The binomial is never printed —
the book says *reindeer* and no more; *tarandus* appears only inside the names of
the flies that live on it ([[taxon:oestrus-tarandi]], [[taxon:oedemagena-tarandi]]).
