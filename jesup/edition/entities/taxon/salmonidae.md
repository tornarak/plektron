## gloss

One of the two fish families the book names, and **it is not the general one**:
chukchi-1-188 sets it against [[taxon:coregonidae]] as a geographical pair — "the mouth
of the Anadyr is the last place to the north to which large shoals of Salmonidæ
resort every year to spawn … On the Arctic shore, Coregonidæ begin to be
abundant from Chaun Bay." Pacific and Arctic, two sentences, two families.

**[[taxon:coregonus]] is deliberately not here**, though chukchi-1-190 names it two
clauses later: that sentence runs "on the Pacific … Salmonidæ …; on the Arctic,
for catching various species of Coregonus", which is chukchi-1-188's split again, so the
Coregonus half is the *contrast* to Salmonidæ and not an instance of it. Modern
taxonomy puts Coregonus inside Salmonidae as a subfamily, and reading it in here
on that ground would be filing the book under a classification it does not use —
its own name for the group is printed, once, and this entry follows the print.

**[[taxon:thymallus-vulgaris]] hangs from [[taxon:pisces]] for a weaker version of the
same reason.** chukchi-1-192 reads "for catching grayling, also various kinds of trout
and other fresh-water species of Salmonidæ", where *other* may govern the trout
alone or the grayling too. One ambiguous clause is not enough to place a genus.
