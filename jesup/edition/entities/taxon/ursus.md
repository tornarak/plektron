## gloss

**Where a bear the page does not colour belongs.** The book names two, and both
have entries — [[taxon:ursus-arctos]] the bear of the forest border, which it
calls *brown* on two pages and *black* on four, and [[taxon:ursus-maritimus]] the
polar bear of the sea-ice. But much of the book says only *bear*: the bear of
1-350's tale who lets the fox heal his wounds with red-hot stones, the one the
Kamchadal track in late spring (1-150), the *reˊkkeñ* that appears bear-sized in
the open (2-023). Those mentions name no species and this entry does not give
them one.

Bare *bear* is left for a reader to route rather than linked here on sight,
because a good many of them are not undetermined at all — they are the polar
bear, carried through a paragraph that named it once and then dropped the
adjective (1-183, 1-184, 2-135). The carcass ceremonial is the hardest of these:
2-135 and 2-136 run the whale and the bear together and colour neither, though
the head-feast they describe is the polar bear's.
