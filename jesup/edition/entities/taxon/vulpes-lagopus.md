## gloss

The book says *white fox* and never *arctic fox*, and sets no binomial for it.
It is the fox of the fur trade — chukchi-1-078's fair returns count 268 of them against
580 red, and chukchi-1-180 reports "seven qualities of white-fox skin are distinguished"
in trade, so most of the fox that appears in the price lists and the tribute
lists is this animal.

*Blue fox* is the same species, its blue morph, priced apart at the fairs
(chukchi-1-078, chukchi-1-079, chukchi-2-109) and not a second kind.

Filed under *Vulpes*: much of the older literature sets this animal as *Alopex
lagopus*, and a reader who came looking under that genus will not find it. The
book itself sets neither.

chukchi-1-179 is where the two foxes are named together, and [[taxon:vulpes-vulpes]] is
the other; bare *fox* stays on [[taxon:vulpes]].
