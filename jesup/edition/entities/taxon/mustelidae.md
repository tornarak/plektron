## gloss

**The family the book meets almost entirely as fur.** Five entries hang here, and
the English names the book sets for them are not interchangeable:
[[taxon:martes-zibellina]] the *sable*, of the Kolyma forest and of the Cossack
tribute; [[taxon:martes-americana]] the *marten*, which Bogoras says at chukchi-1-078
reached the Anyui fair from the American shore and from nowhere in Asia;
[[taxon:mustela-erminea]] the *ermine*, trapped at home and worn as an amulet;
[[taxon:gulo-gulo]] the *wolverene*; and [[taxon:lutrinae]] the *otter*, which the
page never resolves to river or sea.

The book has no word for the family, and nothing in it stands above *sable* and
*marten* as one animal.
