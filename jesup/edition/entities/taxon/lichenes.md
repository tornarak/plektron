## gloss

The reindeer-moss of chukchi-1-102, which is the single most important plant in the
book — the whole winter fodder of the herds, and Bogoras lists seven Chukchee
kinds of it with two Latin names attached ([[taxon:cetraria]],
[[taxon:stenocaulon-paschale]]).

A lichen is a fungus in symbiosis with an alga, so the modern placement is under
[[taxon:fungi]]; Bogoras's own century treated *Lichenes* as a group in its own
right, and calls these mosses.

Note that only two of the seven have a Latin name at all. The other five —
Qai-wattaˊpqai, Koihuˊ-wattaˊp, Mɪˊrg̣ɪčhɪn, Aioˊpɪču-wattaˊp, and the two
colour varieties — are Chukchee folk taxa with no binomial in the print.
