## gloss

The reindeer-moss of chukchi-1-102, which is the single most important plant in the
book — the whole winter fodder of the herds, and Bogoras lists seven Chukchee
kinds of it with two Latin names attached ([[taxon:cetraria]],
[[taxon:stenocaulon-paschale]]).

**Deliberately parentless.** A lichen is a fungus in symbiosis with an alga, so
the modern placement is under [[taxon:fungi]]; Bogoras's own century treated
*Lichenes* as a group in its own right, and calls these mosses. Filing them
either way would be this store choosing between two centuries of botany on no
evidence from the page, so the rank stands alone until an editor rules.

Note that only two of the seven have a Latin name at all. The other five —
Qai-wattaˊpqai, Koihuˊ-wattaˊp, Mɪˊrg̣ɪčhɪn, Aioˊpɪču-wattaˊp, and the two
colour varieties — are Chukchee folk taxa with no binomial in the print, and
they are the [[lexicon]]'s, not this store's.
