## gloss

The sockeye. **chukchi-1-190 sets two names for it and says they are one**: "red salmon
(O. lycaodon or niarka)". Neither is current, and both are aliases here rather
than entries of their own — the page offers them as alternatives for a single
fish, and *red salmon* is what settles which fish that is.
