## gloss

The book says *hare* and sets no binomial. The entry stops at the genus: which
hare it is would be a claim about the fauna of the country rather than a
reading of any page, and no page decides it. chukchi-1-308's *polar hare* is the
book's one qualified form and names no species either.

The animal of the trapping chapter — chukchi-1-182's lighter dead-fall is built for it,
chukchi-1-184's winter battue with rattles and sticks drives it — and of the skins:
the hood of chukchi-1-109, the patch on a white fox-skin at chukchi-1-071, the socks at chukchi-1-308.
In the sacrifice lists of chapter XII it stands beside the fox and the wild
reindeer (chukchi-2-118, chukchi-2-139).

*Hare Mountains* is [[land:hare-mountains]], a range on the Kanchalan, and no
mention of the animal. On chukchi-2-036 a Chukchee name for Cassiopeia is
glossed "group of hares"; the animal is in the etymology only, and Bogoras
says he never heard the constellation called hares rather than reindeer.
