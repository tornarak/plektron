**R8 — schwa-colouring partition (seed).** Small caps track schwa-hood as a
class, not reduction: stressed medial schwa is still ɪ (3), stressed final
schwa still ᴀ (8) — and the printed key glosses ᴀ ᴇ ɪ jointly as "obscure
vowels (short)", so the schwa-class reading is Bogoras's own doctrine, not
merely distributional. The colouring is consonant-conditioned: ᴇ word-initial
and post-geminate; ɪ the default medially and finally; ᴀ only word-finally
after uvular (both tokens) — the clearest signal. ä is the key's "obscure
vowel (long)" — the series' long counterpart, claimed for Chukchi, so far
unattested there (F9).

**The ᵉi convention disambiguates form 10:** were the accent on *i*, Bogoras's
own notation was available (kᵉi´-). He wrote kei´ — prominence on *e*,
consistent with CCKD *kejŋən*.

## 4. Suprasegmentals — evidence and scoring rules

- **Stress:** ≤1 mark per word in all eleven forms; word-final licensed
  (2, 4, 8); no segmental residue anywhere; stress shift under suffixation
  1a→1b. The аӈӄы ~ аӈӄа- stem alternation is the first recurrence specimen
  for harmony testing (ex-F7).
- **Laryngeal ᵋ:** in form 5 sits at modern initial ʔə; pre-consonantal
  position excludes the onglide reading — the positional argument that
  settled the spec's row (F5, closed at corpus scale).
- **R5′ — consonantal glottalization scoring.** Modern ь-type glottalization
  is unmarked throughout this set (unmatched /ʔ/ in 1b, 2, 5, 6 ×2, 7).
  Partial-coverage expectation stands; modern non-glottalized doublets are
  attested, so **score only against items where glottalization is obligatory
  in secondary sources**, doublets excluded; variants column mandatory in any
  crosswalk table.

## 5. Flag register (concordance-side)

**Open:**
- **F2** — plain `n` ~ /ŋ/ in two environments (before lateral, 6;
  prevocalic, 9) against ñ's five clean tokens. Log tokens through the
  harvest; don't resolve.
- **F3** — stressed glottalized schwa written `a` (5). Single token;
  accumulate.
- **F9** — `ä`: the key claims it as the long obscure vowel of the native
  schwa series; the sources checked attest it only in Yupik toponyms. Both
  facts stand. If it proved unlicensed in Chukchi it would move from the
  vowel concordance to the per-language classifier. Test via the harvest;
  no judgement.

(Keying-side flags — F4, F10, F11, the Ģ candidate — live in the spec.)

**Closed, one line each (histories in git):** F1 — h ~ /ɣ/ real in native
words, h loses classifier value; F5 — form 5's superscript is the laryngeal,
position excludes the onglide; F6 — small caps = schwa-hood, corroborated by
key doctrine; F7 — stem alternation filed as harmony specimen (§4); F8 —
k ~ /q/ real variation, k loses classifier value; F12 — the s-palatalization
sort is the dot, the scan's apostrophe shape a print/scan artefact (→ spec
F11); F13 — the laryngeal is genuine, the error was misattaching its gloss to
the ᵉi row; the string "ᵋiᵋ" stays retired.
