Two pairs have now been caught misreading at 2× with **zero felt doubt**, and
they fail the same way: the difference between them is a small structural
detail rather than a quantity of ink, so the crop resolves the letter's shape
well enough to read fluently and not well enough to read correctly.

| pair | seen on | what it looked like |
|---|---|---|
| `ъ` read as `ь` | moll-102, entries 9–13 | five headwords sharing the stem `пуур-` (`пууръу`, `пууръык`, `пууръын`, `пууръыткульын`, `пууръэтык`), every one read `ь` at 2× on both the full page and a batched crop. At 4×: flat top, no bowl, short tail breaking at the baseline — `ъ` in all five. |
| `в` read as `б` | moll-112, entries 35–37 | three glosses on the root `-совывать` — `высо́вывать`, `высо́вывающий`, `засо́вывать/всо́вывать` — all read `б` at 2× *and* 3×. |

**Neither was caught by feeling uncertain**, because neither felt uncertain.
What is available instead is two mechanical triggers, one per side of the row:

- **On the headword side, the cluster is the trigger.** A stem recurring across
  several consecutive entries is not several chances to catch the error — it is
  several chances for the same misreading to pass, each looking exactly as
  confident as the last. When consecutive headwords share a stem ending in what
  looks like `ь` or `ъ`, treat the whole run as one question and zoom it at 4×
  together, even though none of them individually felt doubtful.
- **On the gloss side, the non-word is the trigger.** `высо́бывать` is not a
  Russian word. That, not the letterform, is what sent anyone back to the ink.
  Read your own gloss cell back *as Russian* before finalizing.

**Keep the order of authority straight on the gloss side.** This note's `в`/`б`
half was first written up concluding "a decidable letterform question, not a
language-decides-it question — zoom to 4×," which its own evidence refutes: the
lexicon found the error and the zoom confirmed it. The order matters because it
decides what to do when the two disagree, and on the three occasions 4× has
contradicted a real word or another witness in this pass it has been wrong every
time (moll-042 `ииъэ`, moll-088 `нымлилык'ин`, moll-104 `выве́тривания`). If the
ink at 4× still contradicts a real word, key what you see and mark the row `?`
rather than settling it on either one alone. See [[what-settles-a-letterform]].

The `ъ`/`ь` half is the one case in that note's table where more magnification
genuinely does decide, and it is worth knowing which half you are in. Compare
[[lyn-lyyn-no-felt-doubt]], the same no-doubt shape on the commonest error class
in this book.
