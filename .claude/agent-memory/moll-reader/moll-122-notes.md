count exactly, one wrap at 26. Zoomed roughly 40 of the 48 entries in batches
(all headwords containing `лы`, `ъ`/apostrophe clusters, `-въё` endings) as a
prophylactic check per [[lyn-lyyn-no-felt-doubt]] and

reader has one data point that the prophylactic-zoom habit sometimes just
confirms a clean page rather than always catching something; it does not mean
the check was wasted, only that this particular folio's compositor was careful.

more instances of the bare `(2)` marker (entries 28, 31, 34, 35, 43), always
