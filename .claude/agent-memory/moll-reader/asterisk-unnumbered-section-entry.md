On moll-142, the letter section `Ф` holds exactly one entry — the loanword
`февраль`, glossed `февра́ль` — sitting alone between the `§` heading for `Ф`
and the `§` heading for the next letter, `Ч`. The marked page gives this
bracket the label `*` rather than a numeral, and the context block's `41
entries, numbered 1..41` does not count it: entries 1–36 precede `Ф`, 37–41
follow `Ч`, and the sole `Ф` entry is outside that numbering entirely.

It is not a bracket fault in the [[bracket-faults]] sense (no quire mark, no
merged headwords, no swallowed tail) — it is an ordinary bold flush-left
headword with its own gloss, just one the numbering never reached because a
one-entry section apparently falls outside however the tool assigns
consecutive integers. `zoom STEM:*` reaches it by the same `*` label the
marked page uses.

**How to apply:** give it a row in reading order (it sits after the entries
before its `§` and before the entries after the next `§`), and write
`disagrees:` — the row total will be one over the predicted count, and the
reason is this specific unnumbered entry, not a merged or invented bracket.
Do not invent a numeral for it in the headword cell; the numbering in this
file's `disagrees:` note is enough.
