On moll-046, entry 43 (и'лгын, gloss "правая (основная) постромка оленьей
упряжи"), the gloss word breaks at the line end as "по-" / "стромка". At 2x
and even 4x (the ceiling), the "о" of "по-" prints with enough of a gap on its
right side to look like "с" — i.e. the crop reads as "пс-" rather than "по-".

**Why:** italic serif "о" in this face is not always a closed oval at
letterpress resolution; ink loss on the right stroke produces exactly the
silhouette of "с". Zooming further does not resolve it — the ambiguity is in
the ink, not the crop.

**How to apply:** when a word split by a line-end hyphen would otherwise
resolve to a real, common Russian word (here "постромка", a standard harness
term, corroborated by the surrounding gloss about a reindeer harness), and the
only competing reading produces no real word ("псстромка"), key the real
word and note the ambiguity — do not zoom past the cap chasing it, and do not
leave a `?` when the lexicon plus context genuinely settle it. This is the
same class of corroboration as [[reader vs detector disagreement]]-style
reasoning: the gloss's own Russian is a witness, just like the
headword/ending corroboration table in the pass doc, only here it's applied
inside the gloss itself rather than to the headword.
