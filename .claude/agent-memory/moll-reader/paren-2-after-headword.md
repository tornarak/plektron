A bare `(2)` immediately after the bold headword, before any `1)`/`2)` sense
numbering, is **the book's mark for the strong vowel-harmony row**. Its own
front matter says so:

> Слова с цифрой 2, помещенной в скобках (2), указывают, что они относятся к
> сильному ряду

with the worked example `вэнвытвык (2) доносить, делать донос`. Chukchi has
two harmony sets, and this marks which one the word belongs to. It is a
property of the **word**, not of the definition, which is why there is never a
`1)` to pair it with and why looking for one is wasted effort.

**How to apply:** key it as the first token of the `gloss` cell, ahead of any
`(мн. …)` — `(2) 1) твёрдый; 2) трудный` — exactly as printed. Not appended to
the headword cell, not merged into the following `1)`, not dropped. The one
exception seen so far is moll-075 entry 8, where the print sets it *between*
two comma-joined variants (`лылегылгын, (2) лылечурмын ве́ко`); there it stays
inside the headword cell, because moving it would detach it from the variant it
qualifies.

**This note twice said the meaning was unknown, and that is the part worth
keeping.** Readers meeting the mark have variously called it a homonym numeral
(and keyed it into the headword cell), a compositor error with a missing first
sense, and "print notation of unknown-to-us meaning, possibly a
cross-reference/variant-count marker." Each account was reasoned carefully from
one page. All three were invented, and one of them reached shared memory as
settled fact before a human caught it.

**The book had already answered it in print.** A mark that recurs across pages
in the same position is a *convention*, and a convention is written down —
front matter, the printed alphabet table, the collation rule on folio 3. That
apparatus is evidence and it binds; the reader is not entitled to infer a
meaning for a recurring mark, and the spec's instruction to carry notation
"exactly as printed and unexpanded" is a rule about keying, not a licence to
declare the notation unexplained. When you meet a mark you cannot place, key it
verbatim, say in your report that you could not place it, and let adjudication
read the front matter. Do not write a meaning into memory.
