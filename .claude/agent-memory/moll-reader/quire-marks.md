On moll-034 (folio 33), the marked page numbered bracket 23 over a mark at
the foot of the left column reading "З—561" (a Cyrillic capital letter or
digit, an em-dash, then three digits — the format matches the skill doc's own
"10-561" example). Zoomed at 2x it is plainly not a headword: no bold
Cyrillic word, just the signature mark. Gave it no row, wrote `disagrees:`
even though nothing else about the page was in question — the context block
predicted 43 entries and the correct table has 42 rows.

**Why:** the skill doc already documents this exact failure mode ("A quire
mark got a number") but the specific fact worth keeping is that it happens
even on ordinary, otherwise-unremarkable pages, at the very foot of a column,
and is trivially confirmed at default 2x zoom — no need to go to 4x or agonize
over it.

**How to apply:** any numbered bracket sitting alone at the foot of a column,
especially the last one before a column ends, deserves a quick zoom before
being keyed as an entry. If it's digits/mixed-alnum with a dash and no
Cyrillic headword, it's a quire mark: no row, `disagrees:` with the count.
