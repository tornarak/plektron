On moll-115, entries 17 and 18 share the stem `рыгытко-`: entry 17 is
`рыгыткок * съесть`, entry 18 is `рыгыткук 1) рыть, копать, разгребать; ...`.
At full-page scale both read as ending `-ков`/`-кук` interchangeably — an
initial pass keyed entry 17 as `рыгытков`, inventing a `в` that is not there.
Zoomed to 2x the two are clearly `-ок` and `-ук`, distinct endings, no `в`
anywhere in either word.

This is the same shape as [[what-settles-a-letterform]]'s "two headwords a few
rows apart that look identical at 1x" case (moll-073's `к'эютэкичгын` pair):
neighbouring entries sharing a stem are not independent confirmations of each
other, they are the same misreading risk twice. Crop adjacent same-stem
entries into one frame before finalizing either.

**How to apply:** when two consecutive (or near-consecutive) entries share a
stem, always zoom them together and read the final syllable letter by letter
rather than trusting the page-scale impression that they're "the same word
with a different sense count."
