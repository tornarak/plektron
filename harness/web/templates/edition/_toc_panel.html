{# The printed Contents as a locator, in the sidebar's column. **Not `_toc.html` narrowed.** That partial is the Contents *as a document* — 316 rows set the way the book sets them, leaf marks in the margin, the folio hard right. This is a different question asked of the same store: where am I, and what is around me. So every top-level division is shown, and what stands open is the division you are in, unrolled whole — whole because a chapter opened to the one row that matched shows you the row you already had and hides its neighbours, which is the opposite of a locator. An entry or a form asks a third question, where it is talked about, and `_toc_panel` sends only those rows. **Only what stands open is rendered.** A shut division's sections used to be served too, folded away, on every page: 316 rows, ~77 KB of an ~80 KB entity page. Nothing can be done with them but open the fold and click through, and clicking through is a new page with a panel of its own, open there. So a shut division is a plain row linking to it, and a page carries its open path — 26 rows on a leaf nothing places, ~30–60 on a chapter. The leaf marks are gone: where the printed Contents happened to turn a page is a fact about the binding of the front matter, and it has nothing to say about where you are in chapter XII. **`
