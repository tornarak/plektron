{# The printed Contents, as one list across its seven leaves. Every entry carries its own `toc-{id}` anchor, which is what makes a heading addressable before chapters have pages of their own: `/edition/toc/chI` resolves through the entry's `page_start` today, and will keep resolving unchanged the day chapter I becomes a run. `page_start` links through the folio index and is printed plain where no page witnesses that folio — which is most of the book until collation runs. The number is set hard right, as the book sets it; no dot leaders, which are typesetting rather than text. **The heading is a link too, and is drawn as if it were not.** A reader reaches for the title, not the folio, and a row where only the number is live makes them aim at a two-character target. Unstyled because this is a contents page: colouring 316 rows would turn the list into a wall and say nothing, since every row that can be followed already carries its number as the visible affordance. Where nothing witnesses the folio the title is not a link either — the two are one target and go live together. #}
