## gloss

The people this book is about, in the book’s own spelling. Bogoras divides them
throughout into the Reindeer Chukchee of the interior and the Maritime Chukchee
of the coast, and treats the division as the organising fact of their material
life (1-021).

The bare form is deliberately not auto-linked: “Chukchee” is equally the people,
the language and, in “Chukchee Peninsula”, the place. A reader decides which.
