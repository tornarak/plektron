## gloss

One of "a few tribal names supposed to be Eskimo" that Lütke and Wrangell
report (1-041). Bogoras doubts the supposition: the name "resembles the
Chukchee añqaˊlɪn", as [[people:namollo]] resembles the Koryak word for
maritime settlers. So the entry is a name older travellers used, not a people
Bogoras met. See [[person:wrangell]].
