## gloss

A local division of the Reindeer Chukchee, named by Bogdanovitch for those
"living around St. Lawrence Bay near the river Marich" (1-047). Bogoras adds
that it is "perhaps … a distorted form of the word teg̣reˊtɪlɪt" — the name is
his informant's, not the people's own. Attested once. See [[river:marich]],
[[water:lavrentiya-bay]], [[person:bogdanovich]].
