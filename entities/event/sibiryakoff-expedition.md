## gloss

The expedition of 1894–96 financed by the Siberian gold magnate Alexander
Sibiryakoff, under which Bogoras's Kolyma-district work of 1895–98 was carried
out (1-021). Distinct from event:jesup-expedition, which funded only his second
journey of 1900–01 — the page draws that line itself, and the collator of 1-021
asked for this entry so it could be drawn in the markup too.
