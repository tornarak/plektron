## gloss

The expedition of 1897–1902, financed by Morris K. Jesup and directed by Franz
Boas for the American Museum of Natural History, surveying the peoples of the
North Pacific rim on both the Siberian and American sides. This volume is its
Publications VII. Bogoras states on 1-021 that his own journey of 1900–01
“formed part of” it; his earlier work of 1890–98 did not.
