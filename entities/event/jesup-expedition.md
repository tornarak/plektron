## gloss

The expedition of 1897–1902, financed by Morris K. Jesup and directed by
[[person:boas]] for the American Museum of Natural History, surveying the peoples
of the North Pacific rim on both the Siberian and American sides. This volume is
its Publications VII. [[person:bogoras]] states on 1-021 that his own journey of
1900–01 “formed part of” it; his earlier work of 1890–98 was the
[[event:sibiryakoff-expedition]]’s.
