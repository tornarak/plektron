## gloss

**The book does not name this river, and the entry exists so its basin has a
referent.** 1-359’s “Northern and Adjacent Pacific Shore” list names the
settlement only — `Kiˊčhɪn` against `Кичиги` — and that is
[[place:kichhin]], now the urochishche Старая Кичига. The river is what the
village is named for: the settlement’s coordinate falls inside this basin,
which is the whole of the argument for pairing them.

So every form here is `auto: no` by construction. There is nothing for a rule
to link, because nothing on any page says `Кичига`, and an entry whose forms
appear nowhere in the corpus reads 0 and 0 under `entity occurrences` — which
is the honest shape for a thing the atlas needs and the text never mentions.
