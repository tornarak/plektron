## gloss

The Anadyr's largest tributary, joining it from the south. Bogoras names it
three ways — the Chukchee Maiˊn, the Russian Большая рѣка, and that translated
as "Big River" — which stood as two entries until they were read together.

The two incoming forms are `auto: no` deliberately. "Big River" is an English
phrase before it is a name, and this entry is ratified: an `auto: yes` here
would link every printed occurrence of it corpus-wide on the strength of a
merge nobody has audited. `Maiˊn` is the form the ratification was read
against and keeps its rule.
