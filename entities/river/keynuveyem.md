## gloss

Кейнувеем, on the Arctic coast west of the Kolyma delta. 1-359 lists it under
"The Lower Kolyma" as Keiˊñu-weˊem / Медвѣжья рѣка, with footnote 3: "Chukchee.
The meaning is the same as in Russian" — bear, in both. GeoNames keeps the pair
on one record, `Medvezh'ya` beside `Кейнувеем`, and the neighbourhood confirms
it: `Zaliv Medvezhiy` and `Mys Medvezhiy` sit at its mouth.

**Retitled off the Americanist transcription**, which is the standing rule. The
sweep had missed it by one letter in each direction — `Keiˊñu-weˊem` folds to
`keinuweem` against the gazetteer's `Kejnuveem`, and the store's Cyrillic
`Медвѣжья` had no Cyrillic counterpart on the record, only the Latin. That is
the transliteration-variance class this edition's residue is made of, and it is
why the tool is a proposer and not an adopter.

**`within: river:kolyma` is the page's filing and is not yet tested.** "The
Lower Kolyma" is a country, and its section already produced one river that
drains to the East Siberian Sea on its own rather than to the Kolyma
([[river:large-chukchee-river]], measured at 0.116 contained). The basin for
this one is being fetched; when it lands, `geo verify` will say which this is.
