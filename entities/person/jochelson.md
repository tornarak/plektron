## gloss

Waldemar Jochelson (1855–1937), ethnographer of the Yukaghir and Koryak and
Bogoras’s colleague on the Jesup expedition. Bogoras travels with him on 1-044,
“with W. Jochelson between Gishiginsk…”. The book also cites his publications by
surname alone, which is why the bare form is not auto-linked: the man and the
work are two entities and one string.
