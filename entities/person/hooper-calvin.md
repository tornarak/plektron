## gloss

Author of the *Report of the Cruise of the U. S. Revenue Steamer "Corwin" in the
Arctic Ocean* (1881). **Two men named Hooper are quoted in this book** — the
other is [[person:hooper-william]] — so the bare surname claims nothing
automatically. `data/authorities.json` assigns the siglum *Hooper* to the 1853
work, which is the other one; that is the bibliography's convention and not a
ruling on any given sentence.
