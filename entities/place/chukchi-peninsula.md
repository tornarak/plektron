## gloss

The northeastern tip of Asia, between the Chukchi and Bering Seas.
[[person:bogoras]] travels there in 1900–01 (1-021).

The `area:` is Natural Earth's own `CHUKCHI PENINSULA` region polygon, so the
`geo:` point above is no longer standing in for a boundary — it is kept as the
peninsula's centre and `geo verify` measures it against the shape. Natural
Earth carries `Q1002` for the same feature, which is what corrected the
`wikidata:` link here: it had read `Q193527`, the facial bone.
