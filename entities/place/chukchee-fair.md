## gloss

"In the beginning of the century, simultaneously with a spring fair for the
Reindeer Koryak, was established another one for the southwestern Chukchee on
the Parapolsky Dol, not far from the upper part of the river [[river:opuka]]"
(1-080). The same page later prices "the Chukchee fair on the Opuka River" at
no more than a few thousand rubles a year — **read here as this fair**, the
only one the book puts on the Opuka, though the two sentences do not name each
other.

**What is uncertain is the identity, not the position.** The coordinate above
is the editor's reading of Bogoras's own map, between
[[land:parapolsky-dol]] and the head of the [[river:opuka]] and near enough to
both for the sentence above to describe it. What the map's label names is the
open question — this entry reads it as the Opuka fair, and no page says so in
those words.

**"Chukchee fair" is not a safe form and its alias is `auto: no`.** 1-078 uses
the phrase of the Anui fair — "The Chukchee fair was held at Fort Anuisk" —
which is [[place:ostrovnoye]], not this.
