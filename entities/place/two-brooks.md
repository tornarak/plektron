## gloss

Bogoras's map puts it on the left bank of the [[river:bolshoy-anyuy]], close to
where that river meets the [[river:maliy-anyuy]] and the [[river:kolyma]].
**The exact placement is uncertain**: the coordinate above is the editor's, set
where two brooks do flow into the river, and is a reading of the map rather
than a survey.
