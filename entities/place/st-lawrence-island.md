## gloss

The island in the northern Bering Sea, off the mouth of the [[water:anadyr]]
and about 60 km from the Chukotka coast. The book meets it as the other end of
a trade route: Chukchee crews go over in boats for reindeer-skins, and kettles
and other goods come back from it (1-051, 1-086, 1-104).

**No `within:`**, because the entity above it would be Alaska and this store has
no such entry; the shape locates it and the tree does not need to. Not to be
confused with St. Lawrence Bay on the Chukotka coast (1-047), which is a
different feature and has no entry yet.
