## gloss

Located by the editor from Bogoras's gazetteer (1-048 to 1-053),
cross-referenced against Operational Navigation Charts and OpenStreetMap.
Bogoras + ONC + OSM (latter two as "sirenik").

**Cape Ulakhpen is the same place** and had a separate entry until the book was
read on it. The cape lies northwest of [[water:penkigney-bay]] (1-040); 1-034
says the natives call it Wuteˊen, "the same name as the village close by"; and
1-051 gives that village as "Wuteˊen or Wuˊturen (Eskimo, Čeṛiˊnak), the first
Eskimo village, which lies near Cape Ulakhpen" — Čeṛiˊnak being Sirenik. Its
people are named with those of [[place:chaplino]] and [[place:cape-chukotsky]]
at 1-164.

**Strictly the page distinguishes them** — a headland and a village *close by*,
sharing one native name — and this entry does not, because Bogoras uses the cape
throughout as the name of the locality and two entries a kilometre apart would
split four passages about one people between them. The distinction is kept here
rather than in the store's shape.
