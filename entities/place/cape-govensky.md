## gloss

Kept separate from [[place:cape-olutorsky]] on purpose. Bogoras writes that
"the real Cape Olutorsky lies close to Baron Korff’s Bay (on the Russian maps,
Cape Govensky)" (1-032, repeated in 1-359's note) — an identification he
asserts against the maps, not one this store can confirm. Modern charts have
Мыс Говена and Мыс Олюторский as two capes.

The two entries now sit a few kilometres apart rather than 230, because
[[place:cape-olutorsky]] has been moved to where the book's own map draws it.
**That is the disagreement made visible, not resolved**: they overlap on the
atlas because Bogoras and the modern chart put one name in two places, and
merging them would delete the fact.
