## gloss

A settlement on the [[river:anadyr]] immediately beside
[[place:under-the-cliffs]], a little below the mouth of the [[river:belaya]]
and Ust-Belaya. Bogoras’s map stacks the two labels one above the other on the
same stretch, and 1-048 counts them in one breath — “Chikayeva has two
families, and Under-the-Cliffs three” — adding that the first name has been
borrowed by the Chukchee, which is why a Russian name sits on a Chukchee
settlement. 1-175 has the reindeer leaving the [[land:palpal]] for it.

**No coordinate.** Under-the-Cliffs is at 65.4099 173.4458, and the map puts
this a little west of it — but the crop that shows the two labels carries no
graticule, so “a little west” cannot be turned into a number without inventing
the scale. Adjacency is what the evidence supports and adjacency is what is
recorded.
