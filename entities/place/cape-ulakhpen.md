## gloss

The cape northwest of [[water:provideniya-bay]] (1-040). At 1-034 Bogoras says
the natives call it by the name this store files under [[place:sirenik]]. Its
people are named with those of [[place:chaplino]] and
[[place:cape-chukotsky]] at 1-164.
