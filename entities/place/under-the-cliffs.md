## gloss

Bogoras's map puts it on the curve of the [[river:anadyr]] a little upstream of
the mouth of the [[river:belaya]] and of Ust-Belaya. **The exact placement is
uncertain**: the coordinate above is the editor's, set under the steepest cliff
in that stretch, and is a reading of the map rather than a survey.
