## gloss

The river draining the Anadyr basin into the Bering Sea. Bogoras places
Maritime Chukchee settlements “between the mouth of the Anadyr and Indian Point”
(1-021). The bare form is not auto-linked because the book uses it for the
region and the bay as freely as for the river.
