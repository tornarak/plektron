## gloss

Залив Креста, the inlet north of the mouth of the [[river:anadyr]]. Bogoras
uses it with [[water:kolyuchin-bay]] to delimit the [[land:chukchi-peninsula]]
(1-047), and again as the southern end of the coast the Reindeer Chukchee
occupy (1-098).
