## gloss

Гижигинская губа, the western arm of [[water:penzhina-bay]]. The head of one of
1-359's gazetteer tables. Third of the three things the book calls Gishiga: the
river [[water:gishiga]], the town [[place:gishiginsk]] at its mouth, and this.
