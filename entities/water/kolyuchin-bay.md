## gloss

The bay Bogoras uses, with [[water:kresta-bay]], as the western and southern
limits of the [[land:chukchi-peninsula]] (1-047). The village
[[place:kolyuchin]] "is situated on the small island some distance away from
the narrow mouth of the bay" (1-048) — Bogoras names the island nowhere, so it
has no entry here.
