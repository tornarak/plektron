## gloss

The water between [[place:chukchi-peninsula]] and the Alaskan shore, and the
route the book's traders and boat crews take east.

**No `area:`, deliberately.** No source in hand draws the strait as an extent:
neither Natural Earth 10m gazetteer layer carries it, and hydrographic sources
generally draw a strait as a line between two capes rather than as a polygon.
The point above is Wikidata Q11769's P625 and locates it without claiming a
boundary; `harness geo list` carries the absence as a row with what was sought
and where. `harness entity locate` is what sets an `area:` if a shape is ever
found for it.
