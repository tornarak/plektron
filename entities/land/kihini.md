## gloss

"Kihiˊni Island" (1-048), "an island of the same name" as the village on it
(1-052), "the island of Kihiˊni" (1-160). It carries two of the gazetteer's
villages — [[place:kihini]] on the northwestern shore and [[place:yergin]] at the
southwestern end — and both points fall inside the traced shape, which is the
text and the tracing agreeing without having been asked to.

No gazetteer in hand draws it, so `region:kihini` is the editor's own tracing;
see `data/geo/SOURCES.md` for what that rank means. The bare form is `auto: no`
for the reason [[place:kihini]] sets out.
