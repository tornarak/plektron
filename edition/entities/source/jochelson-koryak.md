## gloss

[[person:jochelson]]’s *The Koryak* — Memoirs AMNH Vol. X, and Vol. VI of the
Jesup series this book is Vol. VII of. **Not in Authorities Quoted**: part 1
went to press in 1904 and the work did not exist yet, so folio 9's asterism —
"For other authorities see references in the text" — is the only provision the
print makes for it. `authority: jochelson1905` points at the record keyed for
that reason, and `edition/authorities.json`'s `source.unprinted` names it.

Parts 2 and 3 cite it 36 times, always as "Vol. VI, Jochelson, The Koryak,
p. NN" — the `Vol. VI` is the *series* volume and stands before the name, which
is why the alias is the two-word siglum and not the volume. **Bare `The Koryak`
is deliberately not an alias**: this book calls the people the Koryak on nearly
every page of part 2, and [[people:koryak]] already claims that form.

Added ahead of the part-2 collation pass rather than out of a keyed page, so
the count above is over `mz/ocr` for the pages nobody has read.
