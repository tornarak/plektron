## gloss

Author of *Ten Months among the Tents of the Tuski* (1853), which
`edition/authorities.json` records under the siglum *Hooper*. **Two men named
Hooper are quoted in this book** — the other is [[person:hooper-calvin]] — so
the bare surname claims nothing automatically. The siglum is the bibliography's
convention; which man a given sentence means is a reading.
