## gloss

1-253: the Maritime Chukchee "eat also several kinds of seaweed which are found on
the seashore. This is gathered only when animal food is scarce, but children eat
it raw while playing on the beach."

**Deliberately parentless**, on [[taxon:lichenes]]'s precedent and for its reason.
*Seaweed* is not a clade: the red, brown and green algae are three lineages that
share no common ancestor to the exclusion of land plants, and the brown algae are
not close to either of the others. Filing the group under [[taxon:plantae]] would
be this store asserting a classification that neither the book nor modern botany
holds, so the rank stands alone until an editor rules.

The page says "several kinds" and names none, so no species hangs from this.
