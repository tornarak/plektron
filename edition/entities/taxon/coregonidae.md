## gloss

The whitefish family, and **the book's own word**: 1-188 opposes it to
[[taxon:salmonidae]] geographically — Salmonidæ spawn up to the mouth of the
Anadyr on the Pacific, "On the Arctic shore, Coregonidæ begin to be abundant
from Chaun Bay, and especially from the mouth of the Kolyma westward." 1-190's
net passage repeats the split, which is why [[taxon:coregonus]] and its five
species sit here and not under Salmonidæ.

Modern taxonomy has folded this family into Salmonidae as the subfamily
Coregoninae. That is a fact about the last century of ichthyology and not about
this page, and the store follows the page.

**This entry existed nowhere in any worklist.** `Coregonidæ` is printed once, in
1-188, and the ligature made it invisible to every token report — see
[[taxon:animalia]] for the sweep that found it.
