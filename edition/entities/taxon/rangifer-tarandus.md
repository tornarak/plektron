## gloss

The animal the book is largely about — the herds of chapter IV, the meat that
opens chapter VIII ("the staple food of the Reindeer Chukchee is reindeer-meat",
1-249), and the division of the whole people into Reindeer and Maritime.

**It had no entry until now, and the reason is worth keeping.** The corpus never
prints `Rangifer`, and never prints `tarandus` except inside the fly-names of
1-108 — [[taxon:oestrus-tarandi]], [[taxon:oedemagena-tarandi]],
[[taxon:tabanus-tarandinus]], all of them named *for* the reindeer rather than
naming it. Zero hits across every keyed page. The store was seeded from the
binomials the book sets, so the one animal it never had to name in Latin was the
one animal it left out. A store built from what a text spells will always be
blindest to what that text takes for granted.

Wild and domesticated reindeer are one species and the book treats them as two
conditions of it; nothing here splits them.
