## gloss

1-252's taboo: the Reindeer Chukchee "abstain from the meat of the wolverene and
black bear, of all the species of [[taxon:canis]], and of most birds of prey."

**Genus only, and deliberately.** No black bear inhabits Chukotka — neither
*U. americanus*, which is North American, nor *U. thibetanus*, whose range stops
far to the south — so the animal on this page is almost certainly a dark-coated
*U. arctos*. That is an inference about the fauna of the region, not a reading of
the page, and the page names no species. Recording the genus keeps the taboo
attached to a real animal without asserting the identification the print does not
make; [[taxon:oedemagena-tarandi]] is the model for why the two must not be
conflated.
