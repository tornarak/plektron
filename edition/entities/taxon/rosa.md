## gloss

1-256, among the substitutes for tea: "leaves of the sweet-brier, of
[[taxon:epilobium-angustifolium]] and of various berries."

**Genus only.** *Sweet-brier* names *R. rubiginosa* in British usage, a plant of
no Siberian occurrence; in accounts of northeastern Asia the word is the ordinary
rendering of the prickly rose, *R. acicularis*. Which one Bogoras had in mind is a
question about his English rather than about the page, and the page says only
"sweet-brier".
