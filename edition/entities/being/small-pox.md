## gloss

Variola. The disease the DISEASES section opens with (1-062): it “has several
times ravaged the population of northeastern Siberia”, latest on the Lower
[[river:kolyma]] in 1884 and the Middle Kolyma in 1889, the second epidemic
falling chiefly on the [[people:russians]] and the [[people:yakut]]. After the
1884 one the Kolyma-tundra camps were left with a few children and “the
masterless herds were scattered” (1-063), the same emptying [[being:measles]]
produced sixteen years later.

Bogoras gives no Chukchee name for it, so `being:` here is where the section's
diseases are filed and not a claim about how this one was reckoned;
[[being:syphilis]] is the one whose name says so outright.
