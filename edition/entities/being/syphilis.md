## gloss

**The one disease in the section whose Chukchee name says in so many words that
it is a Being.** Bogoras parses aˊtal-vaˊɪrg̣ɪn on 1-063 as aˊtal “Chuvantzy” +
vaˊɪrg̣ɪn “substance” — vaˊɪrg̣ɪn being the word 2-040 heads a section with,
“Other ‘Beings’” (vaˊɪrg̣ɪt), and that 1-073 glosses the other way in the same
breath: qaˊa-vaˊɪrg̣ɪn, “literally, ‘reindeer substance’”. The name is *the
Chuvan Being*, and it is what the class of every entry in this directory rests
on.

The etymology is also an aetiology, and Bogoras says so: the Chukchee “regard it
as indigenous”, but the name points at the [[people:chuvans]], “the tribe who in
earlier times were mediators between the [[people:russians]] and the Chukchee,
and probably, after obtaining the disease from the Russians, introduced it among
the Chukchee.”

A syphilitic was unclean — his own bedding, his own dish and bowl, kept aloof.
A family with several among its older members was “subject to shunning” (kuˊrg̣u
lɪˊn˙yo), its fire tainted, refused admission to clean camps “lest the odor of
their hearth and the shadow of their entrance might prove infectious”. Bogoras
met the third stage everywhere — “deformed noses and ugly scars on their
foreheads” — and on the Kolyma the children had a counting-game in which the
winner “immerses” the loser one degree deeper into it, ankle to boot-top.
