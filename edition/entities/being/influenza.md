## gloss

“Another contagious disease, a kind of grippe or influenza, spreads through the
various parts of the country almost every year, claiming scores of victims”
(1-063). Bogoras found it in the [[river:kolyma]] archives a hundred years back
and met it himself two or three times, among the Chukchee, the Russianized
[[people:yukaghir]] and the [[people:itelmen]]: violent cough, headache, and
“affection of various organs and parts of the body, such as deafness in one ear,
partial lameness, sharp pain in the muscles”. It comes from the south and the
west, “and therefore must be considered as coming from the Russian settlements”
(1-064).

**The one disease in the section Bogoras reports being read as a spirit
outright.** In 1896, on the Upper Anui, the Chaun people heard of cases on the
[[river:keperveyem]] and fled the whole western part of the Chaun tundra to
avoid “the spirits seeking for prey”.
